# ref-themed-revlights  (widget set)
Reference: refs/ref-themed-revlights.png

## Purpose
Design target for BROAD per-car themed rev-light / shift-light variants. Each mimics the real on-wheel / dash-top
shift-light LAYOUT & color signature of that car family (NOT just a recolor) — distinct segment counts, arrangements
and ramps. NO official logos/marks (shape + color inspiration only).

## American-English prompt (validated)
High-fidelity sim-racing shift-light REFERENCE SHEET on pure black, six distinct car-inspired rev-light bar styles
stacked, each a flat front-on emissive LED indicator, no wheel, no car, no logos: (1) FERRARI-STYLE — a wide arc of
round LEDs sweeping green to red with bright blue shift LEDs at the very top-center. (2) PORSCHE-CUP-STYLE — a straight
row of rectangular segments, amber to red, with two white shift flashers at the ends. (3) MERCEDES-AMG-STYLE — three
grouped clusters (left green, center yellow, right red) of chunky bars with a bold center gap. (4) McLAREN-STYLE — a
thin continuous gradient strip, teal-green to papaya-orange to red, minimal. (5) CORVETTE-STYLE — dual stacked LED rows,
green to red, with a large central number gap. (6) LAMBORGHINI-STYLE — an angular hexagon-segment bar, green to violet
to red, aggressive. Pure black transparent-looking background, NO frames, NO borders, thin gray caption under each row.
Emissive neon-on-black, crisp, high contrast. US English only. No brand logos or text marks.

## Checklist notes
- Subject: 6 car-family-inspired shift-light layouts (Ferrari/Porsche/Mercedes-AMG/McLaren/Corvette/Lamborghini).
- Data/values: green->red ramps + blue/white shift flash; each layout structurally distinct.
- Layout/sizing: 6 stacked full-width rows, compact.
- Theme: real-car on-wheel/dash shift-light signatures (shape + color only, no logos).
- Color rules: green low -> amber/red high -> blue or white = shift now; per-car accent (papaya, violet, etc.).
- AVOID (app output): no titles, transparent bg, no borders, NO brand logos/marks (captions are guide-only).
